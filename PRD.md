## 🎯 Vision

Mengubah Hermes Vision OS menjadi **futuristic AI Agentic Operating System** dengan visual yang memukau, interaktif, dan immersive seperti antarmuka di film sci-fi (Iron Man Jarvis, Ready Player One, Blade Runner 2049).

---

## 🎨 Design Direction

### **Theme: Cyberpunk Futuristic**
- **Primary**: Dark space background dengan gradient deep navy → black
- **Accent**: Neon cyan (#00D4FF), neon purple (#8B5CF6), neon green (#10B981)
- **Effects**: Bloom, glow, holographic, scanlines, particle systems
- **Typography**: Orbitron (headings), Inter (body), JetBrains Mono (code)

### **Visual Elements**
1. **Holographic Cards** - Glassmorphism dengan border glow
2. **Animated Grid Background** - Moving tech grid pattern
3. **Scanline Effect** - Subtle CRT monitor feel
4. **Particle Systems** - Floating data particles
5. **Neon Borders** - Glowing edges pada semua cards
6. **3D Elements** - Depth dan perspective transforms

### **Frontend Stack**
```
- React 18 + TypeScript
```
